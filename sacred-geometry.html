Mathematical invariants
The circle-center network is a contemporary line construction; it is not attributed here to an ancient source. The Fibonacci construction uses integer square sizes; its finite spiral is not an exact logarithmic golden spiral.

Historical interpretations and regional variations
Shapes are mathematically reusable across traditions, but the names, meanings, and practices are not interchangeable. Dates below describe documented uses or scholarly contexts, not when a geometric construction was first invented.
Cultural attributions are contextual notes—not part of the core Trochos grammar. Modern umbrella labels such as “sacred geometry” should not be projected backward as ancient universal terminology.
