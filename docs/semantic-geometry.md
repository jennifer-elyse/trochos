# Trochos 2.0 — Emotional semantic sacred geometry

## Goal
Trochos is a language of emotions, actions, concepts and relationships, not an atlas of historical sacred-geometry icons. Version 2.0 keeps the 1.x glyph catalog, trochoid generators, animation and geometry-coupled colors, and adds a restrained sacred-geometry relationship per semantic family.

## Semantic transforms
- Union: Love, Together, Belong, Trust, Compassion, Give, Receive use two equal circles whose centers are one radius apart. This constructs a mathematically exact vesica-like intersection; it means shared domain *only within the invented Trochos visual grammar*.
- Inward: Self, Being, Calm, Know, One, Order, Object, Space use concentric circles at ratios 1 and 1/√3.
- Opening: Hope, Wonder, Future, Freedom, Seek, Start, Surprise use a deliberately incomplete arc plus a small triangle, indicating openness.
- Tension: Fear, Anger, Conflict, Shame, Grief, Apart, Longing, Stop use an open circle and a slightly offset triangle.
- Motion: Time, Change, Move, Present, Past, End, Remember, Forget use displaced partial arcs and one triangle.
- Plurality: Many, Quantity, Other, Pride, Joy, Speak use an outer circle and three understated arcs.
- Remaining words use the inward motif and the two original poetic compositions use union as a default; glyph recipes and compositions remain unchanged.

These meanings are authored design conventions, **not universal psychological or cultural claims**. Geometry stays subordinate to each glyph's original trochoid curves, and scales and rotates with the component.

## Chromatic treatment
Retains the 1.1 line-derived OKLCH-to-sRGB approximation and observer-projection controls. No independently assigned spiritual colors.

## Backward compatibility
The immutable archived version remains in versions/1.1/index.html, with its own copy of data/glyphs.json. Keep the 1.1 archive immutable and expose a separate side-by-side, blend, and pixel heatmap diff page. Never replace the emotional viewer with the independent sacred-geometry laboratory.

## Cultural variations
Keep historical contexts on a separate expandable track. Euclidean constructions, Christian mandorlas, regional Islamic ornament and South Asian practice-specific yantras have distinct histories, and resemblance does not imply identity of origin or meaning. Historical dating and attribution should be independently corroborated, and modern terms should not be projected backward.

## Release policy
2.0.0-beta.2 is scoped to the sacred-geometry branch. Production main remains 1.1 until explicitly merged.
